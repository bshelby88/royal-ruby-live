# Royal Ruby ecosystem — current operating state

Royal Ruby currently has one active customer rail: the free checklist and product-interest signup at `https://royalruby.io`, measured through the existing Formspree form.

## Active

- Educational landing page and printable checklist.
- Product-interest values captured with email submissions.
- Affiliate and UTM attribution preserved in form submissions.
- Privacy-friendly site analytics.

## Disabled

- Paid checkout products have no configured rail or URL.
- NFT and Farcaster transaction actions are disabled.
- No verified contract address, price, supply, royalty, holder benefit, treasury flow, or deployment is represented as live.

## Activation rule

A future commercial rail requires separate verification of the provider/account, fulfillment, legal copy, exact HTTPS hostname allowlisting, and end-to-end tests. Until all are complete, customer-facing language remains waitlist-only or Coming Soon.

This document describes current reality, not a launch promise or historical roadmap.
