# Royal Ruby — posting playbook

## Current destination

Send all audience traffic to `https://royalruby.io` and lead with the free checklist. Product pages are waitlist-only. The digital collectible and Farcaster surfaces are Coming Soon and must not be described as deployed, purchasable, priced, or supply-limited.

## Seven-day publishing loop

1. Publish one practical credit-education clip.
2. Link to the free checklist with a tagged source URL.
3. Reply to questions without promising score outcomes.
4. Review Vercel analytics and Formspree submissions.
5. Compare visits, checklist signups, and product-interest values by UTM source.
6. Repurpose the strongest educational post.
7. Record learnings and plan the next week.

## Approved claims

- The free checklist is available.
- Product-interest signup is available through the website form.
- Paid products are not currently available for checkout.
- Digital collectibles are in development; no verified contract or purchase action is published.

## Measurement

Preserve `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, and affiliate `ref` values. Evaluate traffic → Formspree signup and traffic → product-interest signup. Do not report paid conversion or on-chain activity while those rails are disabled.
